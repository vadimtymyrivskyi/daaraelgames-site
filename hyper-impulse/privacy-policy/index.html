Privacy Policy

Hyper Impulse: Bricks & Balls · Daarael Games · Version 2 · Published 16 October 2026 · In effect from 23 October 2026

This policy explains what happens to data when you play Hyper Impulse: Bricks & Balls ("the Game"), published by Daarael Games, the trading name of Vadym Tymyrivskyi, an individual entrepreneur registered in Ukraine ("we", "us"). We are the controller of the personal data described below, except where we say that Google is. You can reach us at daaraelgames@gmail.com; we will give our postal address on request, without delay.

The short version

We do not ask you for your name, email, or any account. Your game progress is saved on your device (and, if you use it, in Android's own backup to your Google account), not on a server of ours. Data leaves your phone in two ways: automatically, to the Google services built into the Game and described below — one of them, analytics, works for us; and only if you choose to, when you write to us. We do not sell personal data.

What reaches us directly

Nothing automatically. The Game has no sign-up, no login and no server of our own. Your progress, settings, purchases and collected items are stored locally on your device. The analytics described in "Google services in the Game" is collected and held by Google for us, and we see it only in Google's reports, which do not show your name or contact details.

If you write to us

The Game's Send Feedback button opens your own email app with a message to us that already contains the Game's version, your device model and your Android version, so that we can understand a problem without asking you for them. Nothing is sent until you press send, and you can delete those lines first. When you do write to us, we receive your email address, those technical details and whatever you choose to include. We use them only to read and answer your message and to fix the problem you report, and we keep them only for as long as that takes and for any follow-up — then we delete them. We never add you to a mailing list and never share your message with anyone.

Google services in the Game

The Game includes three Google services. They collect data automatically while the Game runs:

  • Google AdMob — shows ads inside the Game. It may access your device's advertising identifier, IP address, device type and operating system, and information about the ads you were shown or interacted with, in order to select and measure ads and to prevent ad fraud. Before ads load, the Game also asks Google whether its consent form applies to you; Google decides this from your approximate location, based on your IP address. For this data Google is an independent controller: it decides how the data is used, under its own policies, and may share it with advertisers and other ad technology companies that take part in its ad auctions. How Google uses data from apps that use its services
  • Google Analytics for Firebase — tells us how many people play and where the Game is too hard or too easy. For this data we are the controller and Google processes it on our behalf. It collects by itself, among other things: when the Game is first opened, used, updated or removed, and for how long it is played; completed in-app purchases with their price; ad impressions, clicks and rewards, with the ad revenue where it is available; the device model, operating system, Game version and language; an app instance identifier and the advertising identifier; and your approximate location (country, region and city), estimated from your IP address. The Game adds what happens in it: when the main menu first appears after launch; levels started and finished (the result, stars, rounds played, time, which attempt it was, revives, blocks left, the number of balls and how many upgrades you own), level milestones reached, each revive taken, star chests opened and what they gave, balls unlocked and how, daily rewards claimed, visits to the shop and upgrade screens, gems and HyperCoins earned and spent (how many, where from and what for), which offer a purchase was made from, and rewarded ads offered and watched. It also keeps a few facts about your progress: your highest level, total stars, your number of upgrades, whether you own No Ads or Pro Aim, and whether you have ever made a purchase. It does not receive your name or contact details. Google uses this data to provide the analytics service to us. Under our account's data-sharing settings, it may also use the data in aggregated form, combined with data from other apps so that no one can be identified, for statistics on industry trends and for benchmarking. Firebase privacy
  • Google Play — handles purchases (see "Purchases"), and shows Google's own in-app rating card and in-app update prompts. These are Google's screens: what you do in them is handled by Google as an independent controller under its own terms; the Game only learns whether a purchase completed. Google Privacy Policy

Why the data is used

  • To show ads, which keep the Game free, and to give you the rewards you choose to watch an ad for.
  • To improve the Game — to see which levels are too hard, where players stop playing, and whether the economy and the shop are fair.
  • To deliver and restore purchases.
  • To measure our own advertising of the Game — for example, how many players an ad campaign brought and how far they got — using Google's advertising tools.
  • To answer you when you write to us.

We do not use the data for any other purpose, and we do not make decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects. The only automated selection is the one Google makes when it chooses which ad to show (see "Personalised ads").

Legal basis. Where the law asks for one, we rely on our legitimate interest in providing, funding and improving the free Game and in answering you. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, analytics and personalised ads rely on your consent instead (see "Information for specific countries").

Whose data, and where it comes from. The data is about players of the Game and people who write to us. It comes from your device, through the Google services in the Game, and from the emails you send. We do not process sensitive data, such as data about health, religion, political opinions, biometrics or precise location.

Do you have to provide any data?

No law or contract requires you to give us anything. Writing to us is entirely your choice; if you do not, we simply cannot answer you. The data collected by Google's services is part of how the free Game is provided. Outside the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland all of it is collected whenever the Game runs. Inside them, if you refuse in the consent form, analytics stays off and ads are not personalised, but Google still receives the data it needs to show and count non-personalised ads. The only way to avoid it entirely is not to use the Game. You can always limit personalised ads (see below); doing so does not change anything in the Game.

Where the data goes

The data the Game collects automatically goes to Google. For advertising, Google may in turn share data with advertisers and other ad technology companies, under its own policies (see "Google services in the Game"). Details of the transfer to Google:

  • Who: Google LLC, 1600 Amphitheatre Parkway, Mountain View, CA 94043, USA, and its affiliates. Contact: Google's privacy contact form.
  • Where: the United States and the other countries where Google has data centres.
  • When and how: repeatedly while the Game is running and connected to the internet, over encrypted connections.
  • What and why: the data listed in "Google services in the Game", for the purposes in "Why the data is used".
  • For how long: see "How long data is kept, and how it is deleted".
  • Safeguards: Google LLC is certified under the EU–U.S. and the Swiss–U.S. Data Privacy Frameworks and the UK Extension, and uses the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses where they are required. Google's data transfer frameworks
  • How to refuse, and what follows: see "Do you have to provide any data?" and "Personalised ads".

An email you send us is held in our email account, provided by Google, and is not passed to anyone else.

Personalised ads

Ads in the Game may be personalised: Google may choose them using your device's advertising identifier and your activity in other apps and websites, to show ads that are more likely to interest you. This has no effect on the Game itself. You can limit it:

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the Game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown and before analytics is turned on, using Google's consent form. You can change that choice at any time from the Game's Settings screen.
  • Anywhere, on Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact path may differ on your phone). Doing so limits ad personalisation across all apps. Ads will still appear, but they will no longer be selected using your prior activity.

Identifiers on your device

Google's services in the Game use identifiers stored on your device: the advertising identifier (used by AdMob and by Firebase) and an app instance identifier (used by Firebase). Ads are displayed by Google and may use cookies or similar technologies inside the ad itself. You can reset or delete the advertising identifier as described above; the app instance identifier is deleted when you uninstall the Game or clear its data.

Do Not Track

The Game does not respond to "Do Not Track" signals, because Android apps do not receive them. Google, as the third party in the Game, may collect data about your activity over time and across different apps and websites; the ways to limit this are listed in "Personalised ads".

Purchases

If you buy something in the Game, the payment is handled entirely by Google Play. We never receive or store your card details. The Game only learns that a purchase was completed, so that it can grant what you bought; analytics also records the purchase and its price (see "Google services in the Game").

Children

The Game is not directed at children under the age of 13. Its target audience on Google Play is set to 13 and over, and it is not tagged for child-directed treatment in Google AdMob. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided data through the Game, write to us at the address below and we will help you have it removed by the service that holds it. A parent or guardian can exercise a child's rights under this policy on the child's behalf.

Backups by Android

Android can back up apps' data to your Google account and restore it when you reinstall an app or set up a new phone. If this is turned on in your phone's settings, the Game's local data may be included. That backup is stored by Google in your account, not by us; you control it in your phone's backup settings.

How long data is kept, and how it is deleted

  • Emails you send us — kept only as long as needed to deal with them (see "If you write to us"), then deleted from our mailbox and its trash.
  • On your device — your progress and settings stay in the Game's local storage for as long as the Game is installed. Uninstalling it, or clearing the app's data in Android settings, removes them immediately and permanently.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase — Google deletes event data automatically 2 months after it is collected. Data linked to the app instance identifier or the advertising identifier is deleted 14 months after the Game was last used on the device; each new use starts those 14 months again. Google runs these deletions once a month, so data may stay up to a month longer. Aggregated reports that do not identify anyone may be kept longer.
  • Google AdMob and Google Play — retained by Google under its own retention policy; we have no access to that data and cannot delete it on your behalf.

We keep no paper records, and no personal data is kept after its period ends for any other reason.

How the data is protected

We hold almost nothing ourselves: the only personal data that reaches us directly is an email you chose to send us, kept in an email account that only we can access. Your saved progress stays on your device and in your own Android backup. Analytics data is held by Google on our behalf, and we see it only in Google's reports. The data handled by Google is protected by Google's own security measures, and it travels over encrypted connections.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have some or all of these rights: to know whether we process data about you and to get a copy of it; to have it corrected or completed; to have it deleted; to restrict or object to its processing; to receive it in a portable form; to withdraw consent at any time, without affecting processing that already took place; not to be treated differently for using these rights; and to complain to a data protection authority.

How to use them. Write to us at daaraelgames@gmail.com — it is free. Because the Game has no accounts, the only data we can link to you is an email you sent us, so please write from the same address. We will answer without undue delay and within the time limit set by the law that applies to you. Data collected by Google's services is not linked to your name, and we cannot look it up by your email; to delete what the Game has stored:

  • uninstalling the Game, or clearing its data, deletes all local progress and the app instance identifier; analytics already collected is then no longer linked to your device and is deleted at the end of its retention period;
  • resetting or deleting your advertising ID means new activity can no longer be linked to data collected under the old one;
  • for data Google holds as an independent controller, use the links above or your Google account controls — write to us and we will point you to the right place.

The person responsible for personal data at Daarael Games is Vadym Tymyrivskyi, daaraelgames@gmail.com.
