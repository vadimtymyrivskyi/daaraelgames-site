Privacy Policy

Hyper Impulse: Bricks & Balls · Daarael Games · Last updated: 3 October 2026

This policy explains what happens to data when you play Hyper Impulse: Bricks & Balls ("the Game"), published by Daarael Games, the trading name of Vadym Tymyrivskyi, an individual entrepreneur registered in Ukraine ("we", "us"). We are the controller of the personal data described in "What we collect ourselves" below; you can reach us at daaraelgames@gmail.com.

The short version

We do not ask you for your name, email, or any account. We never see your game progress — it stays on your device (and, if you use it, in Android's own backup to your Google account). Data leaves your phone in two ways: automatically, to Google's advertising, analytics and Google Play services described below; and only if you choose to, when you write to us.

What we collect ourselves

Nothing automatically. The Game has no sign-up, no login and no server of our own. Your progress, settings, purchases and collected items are stored locally on your device and are not transmitted to us.

If you write to us

The Game's Send Feedback button opens your own email app with a message to us that already contains the Game's version, your device model and your Android version, so that we can understand a problem without asking you for them. Nothing is sent until you press send, and you can delete those lines first. When you do write to us, we receive your email address, those technical details and whatever you choose to include. We use them only to read and answer your message and to fix the problem you report, and we keep them only for as long as that takes and for any follow-up — then we delete them. We never add you to a mailing list and never share your message with anyone.

What third parties collect

The Game includes software from Google. These services may collect data automatically:

  • Google AdMob — shows ads inside the Game. It may access your device's advertising identifier, IP address, device type and operating system, and information about the ads you were shown or interacted with, in order to select and measure ads. How Google uses data
  • Google Firebase Analytics — counts app opens and sessions, and records what happens in the Game so that we can see how many people play, how hard each level is and where players stop: levels started and finished (result, stars, rounds, time played, revives used), rewards collected, items unlocked, gems and coins spent, visits to the shop, completed in-app purchases (the product and its price), ads shown, and rewarded ads offered and watched. It also keeps a few facts about your progress — your highest level, total stars, and whether you bought No Ads. It may estimate your approximate location from your IP address and may use device identifiers and diagnostic data. It does not receive your name or contact details. Firebase privacy
  • Google Play — handles purchases (see below), and shows Google's own in-app rating card and in-app update prompts. These are Google's screens: what you do in them is handled by Google under its own terms, and we only learn whether a purchase completed. Google Privacy Policy

These companies act as independent controllers of the data they collect. Their own policies apply in addition to this one.

Advertising choices

Ads in the Game may be personalised, meaning they are selected using data about your device and prior activity.

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the Game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google's consent form. You can change that choice at any time from the Game's Settings screen.
  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Privacy → Ads. Doing so limits ad personalisation across all apps.

Purchases

If you buy something in the Game, the payment is handled entirely by Google Play. We never receive or store your card details. We only learn that a purchase was completed, so that the Game can grant what you bought.

Children

The Game is not directed at children under the age of 13. Its target audience on Google Play is set to 13 and over, and it is not tagged for child-directed treatment in Google AdMob. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided data through the Game, write to us at the address below and we will help you have it removed by the service that holds it.

Backups by Android

Android can back up apps' data to your Google account and restore it when you reinstall an app or set up a new phone. If this is turned on in your phone's settings, the Game's local data may be included. That backup is stored by Google in your account, not by us; you control it in your phone's backup settings.

How long data is kept

The only thing we ever hold is an email you chose to send us, kept only as long as needed to deal with it (see "If you write to us").

  • On your device — your progress and settings stay in the Game's local storage for as long as the Game is installed. Uninstalling it, or clearing the app's data in Android settings, removes them immediately and permanently.
  • Google Firebase Analytics — event and device data is deleted by Google after the retention period configured for this app in our Firebase settings, counted from each user's last activity.
  • Google AdMob — advertising data is retained by Google under its own policies, which you can read at the links above; we have no access to it and cannot delete it on your behalf.

Your rights

Apart from an email you may have sent us, we hold no data about you. If you wrote to us, you can ask us at the address below to see or delete your messages. To remove everything the Game has stored:

  • uninstalling the Game deletes all local progress from your device;
  • for data held by Google, use the links above or your Google account controls.

If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland

Where the GDPR applies, the data described above is processed on these legal bases:

  • Your consent (Article 6(1)(a)) — for personalised advertising and for analytics that rely on device identifiers. You give or refuse it in the consent form shown when you first open the Game, and you can change your answer at any time from the Game's Settings screen.
  • Legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)) — for showing non-personalised advertising, for preventing fraud and abuse of ad rewards, for keeping the Game working and diagnosing crashes, and for answering emails you send us.

You have the right to ask for access to your data, and for it to be corrected, erased or restricted; to object to processing based on legitimate interests; to receive your data in a portable form; and to withdraw consent at any time without affecting processing that already took place. For emails you sent us, write to us and we will act on your request. For data held by Google, the requests go to Google, through the links above or your Google account controls — write to us and we will point you to the right place.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your national data protection supervisory authority.

Transfers outside your country. Google processes data in the United States and in other countries where it operates. Those transfers rely on the safeguards Google puts in place for them, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

If you are in California

We do not sell your personal information, and we never have. However, under the CCPA as amended by the CPRA, showing personalised ads counts as "sharing" personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising.

You may opt out of that sharing at any time. Because the Game has no account and we hold no data about you, the opt-out is made on your device and applies to every app on it: open Settings → Privacy → Ads and turn on Opt out of Ads Personalisation (or delete your advertising ID). Ads will still appear, but they will no longer be selected using your prior activity.

You also have the right to know what personal information is collected and to ask for its deletion or correction, and the right not to be treated differently for exercising any of these rights. On our side we hold only emails you chose to send us — ask us about those directly; requests about other data are directed to Google using the links above, and we will help you find the right form.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version appears on this page with a new date. Continuing to play after that means the updated policy applies.
